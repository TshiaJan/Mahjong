# Privacy Policy for Mahjong Tile Match

**Effective Date:** September 17, 2026  
**Developer:** Tshiamo Jantjie  
**Contact:** janaire.dev@gmail.com  

Thank you for playing **Mahjong Tile Match** ("the Game", "we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal data. This Privacy Policy details our policies regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure when you play the game.

---

### 1. Information We Do Not Collect
- **No Personal Identifiable Information (PII):** We do not require accounts, logins, or personal profile data such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, or payment card information.
- **No Location Tracking:** We do not track, collect, or access your GPS or physical location data.
- **No Device Intrusion:** We do not access your device contacts, private media, camera, or microphone.

---

### 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The Game operates primarily client-side and retains your gameplay progress and preferences locally on your device via standard browser or webview local storage (`localStorage`). This data includes:
- **Game Progression:** Current level number, completed levels, and star ratings.
- **Pretend Virtual Economy:** Token balance earned during gameplay (tokens are purely cosmetic/virtual and hold no real monetary value).
- **Player Preferences:** Sound effects on/off toggles and initial welcome/tutorial completion flags.

*Note: This data is stored strictly on your local device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its cache will reset this local progress.*

---

### 3. Advertising & Third-Party Services
The Game is fully playable offline. When connected to the internet, optional third-party advertising partners (such as Google AdMob or Unity Ads) may be integrated to serve non-intrusive or optional rewarded video advertisements (e.g., to earn virtual in-game tokens).

These third-party ad networks may process pseudonymous, non-personally identifiable diagnostic information in compliance with their own privacy policies, including:
- Mobile Advertising Identifiers (Google Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
- General device specifications (device model, operating system version, carrier/connection type).
- Ad delivery and performance metrics.

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of interest-based advertising at any time through your device settings (*Settings > Google > Ads* on Android or *Settings > Privacy > Tracking* on iOS).

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
**Mahjong Tile Match** is a classic casual puzzle designed for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 where required by local law). If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us at **tshiajan@gmail.com**, and we will promptly delete it.

---

### 5. Data Security
Since we do not operate remote user databases or collect personal user profiles, your data remains secure within your operating system's standard application sandbox.
